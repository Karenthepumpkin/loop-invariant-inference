```
exists p_prev, dllseg_shape(l, prev, p_prev, p) * dlistrep_shape(p, p_prev)
```

- `dllseg_shape(l, prev, p_prev, p)`: the segment of already-traversed nodes from l to just before p
- `dlistrep_shape(p, p_prev)`: the remaining untraversed list starting from p
- The `exists p_prev` connects the two parts as a gap-node pointer

Key learnings:
1. `prot != 0` in PROP caused `entail_wit_1` failure because the invariant must also hold for empty lists (l=0)
2. Using field expressions (`p->prev`) directly in predicate args caused `dup_data_at_error` because the same field appeared in both predicates
3. The simple `exists` approach without PROP constraints worked perfectly — no `p != 0` needed since the loop condition guards it
